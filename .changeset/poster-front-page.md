A poster front page. A page can now open with "Poster": its title very large and light on a full-width coloured field, with the lead beside it. A soft hyphen in a one-word title breaks it there and sets the second half in italic. The archive block gains three layouts to go with it: "Poster" (the first story on a large card that slides up over the field), "Numbered" (a very large heading over numbered titles) and "Band" (one story on a full-width coloured band), and a "Skip" number so a block can leave out the stories a block above already shows.
