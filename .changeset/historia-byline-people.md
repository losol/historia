The byline under an article's lead shows its people: each with their picture, or their initials when there is none, their name, linked to their page, and what they did, from the contributor's role ("Tekst", "Foto"). The avatar is ratio-ui's `Avatar` (2.41), hidden from screen readers since the name is next to it. It was one line of names before, styled with a class the site's CSS did not have.
