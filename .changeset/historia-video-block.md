A video block for lessons and courses: paste the link to a video on YouTube or Vimeo, and give it a title. The link is checked when the lesson is published, and a start time in it is kept. On the site the video is not loaded until the reader presses play, so the page sends nothing to YouTube or Vimeo before then; YouTube plays from its no-cookie domain and Vimeo with Do Not Track. A European or open-source service for uploads and closed courses is meant to follow (ADR 0012, open question 4).
