Courses on the site. A course has a page at `/no/c/kurs/{slug}--{id}` (`/en/c/courses/…`) with its story, its syllabus and a button to start, and each lesson has a page below it, with previous and next. Lessons that are not published are left out. A course for enrolled participants shows its page and syllabus, and keeps its lessons closed until enrolment exists. Courses and lessons can be previewed from the admin, courses are in the sitemap and the search index (with a migration), and `/no/c/kurs` lists them.
