New package: a small client for reading a Historia website's published products from other sites. Plain TypeScript without runtime dependencies, for browsers, Node and edge runtimes. `products.list`, `products.get` (by slug) and `products.getById`, with prices, images and the product's shop URL in a small public shape, `HistoriaError` for failed requests and `formatPrice` for minor units.
