Courses and lessons in the admin, as two new collections with their migration. A small course is one document; a larger one has a syllabus of sections with lessons, made from the course page, and a lesson can be used in several courses. Published courses are in the API, lessons only for people with a role on the website. There are no course pages on the site yet: the routes, video and questions, enrolment and progress follow in the steps of ADR 0012.
