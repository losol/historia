Login through any OpenID Connect provider, such as Keycloak (Tessera), configured with `OIDC_LOGIN_ENABLED`, `OIDC_ISSUER`, `OIDC_CLIENT_ID`, `OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET`, `OIDC_ALLOW_SIGNUP` and `OIDC_LOGIN_LABEL`. The routes are `/api/auth/oidc/*`, and a button appears on the admin login.
